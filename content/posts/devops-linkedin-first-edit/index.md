I’m a full-time nurse, and I’m teaching myself DevOps. 🚀  
Two months ago I started the KubeCraft program to turn a long-standing passion for technology into a career. Since then I’ve worked hands-on with Linux, Git, Docker, and Kubernetes (k3s), and every course makes the path into tech clearer.  
This week I also rebuilt my LinkedIn profile so it reflects where I’m heading, not just where I’ve been.  
Nursing hasn’t gone anywhere. I’m still on the ward, and the habits carry over more than I expected: staying calm when things go wrong, documenting carefully, handing over clearly, and working as part of a team. Those sound a lot like good incident response.  
Over the coming weeks I’ll share what I’m building, what I’m learning, and what breaks along the way.
